For each case record actual output, not just the final action. A correct action with a wrong amount, unsupported reason, wrong source or duplicate decision is a failure. Connection failure, missing setup or unavailable capability is Blocked until investigated, not a pass.

Advanced fault cases (process death exactly after commit, real deadlines and simultaneous writes) are in the automated Gate V suite. A manual Ctrl+C restart does not prove a precise commit-boundary case. LIVE evaluation and timing are separate evidence and may remain unqualified even when every STUB walkthrough passes.
